~/about

A designer who writes the code, so nothing gets lost in the handoff.

Weekflow started as a planner for my own week. It's still in daily use, mine and a few coworkers' who now run real day-to-day operations through it, and every complaint that's come up has gone back into the design.

That's the loop I care about: ship it, watch real people use it, fix what's actually broken rather than what just looks broken. It's the same standard I hold every concept to before it's ever built.
